# 0.5 — The working day runs in nocx

Charter, approved by the owner on 2026-09-19 (nocx-q8yjf.5). The milestone itself was
declared by the backlog cleanup of 2026-09-17; this records what it is for, what is out
and how much unplanned work it takes. Status is never kept here — ask the tracker:
`scripts/feature-status.sh <feature>`.

## What ships

The owner's whole working day runs in nocx: herdr is uninstalled, and nothing sends the
owner to another terminal.

## Outcomes

Independent. Their order here is not a dependency; priority is set in the tracker.

1. **The owner's working day runs in nocx, and herdr is uninstalled** (nocx-xn63t). The
   agents run on the VM, the owner attaches from either laptop, each worker gets its own
   worktree, pane indicators tell working, blocked and finished apart truthfully, and
   agents message each other. **Check:** one full working day with herdr stopped
   (nocx-xn63t.6.2), on top of each stage's own criterion.
2. **A working day in nocx's terminal never sends you to another terminal** (nocx-fxzot).
   **Check:** the owner runs a full working day in nocx, does not open another terminal
   once, and says so — a judgement, deliberately, as recorded on the feature. Three
   stages:
   - the backend owns the blocks (nocx-2v80t);
   - the client paints cells and sends intent, and xterm.js is gone (nocx-zg3k3);
   - the bugs the owner hits in a working day are fixed on the new render path
     (nocx-fxzot.1).

**The foundation comes first — the owner's decision of 2026-09-19.** The first two stages
of outcome 2 and all their tasks are priority 0. That is priority, not a blocking edge:
outcome 1 is not held behind them, and where the two collide on the same code a leaf-level
edge says so.

## Out

What somebody will assume is in, and is not:

- The phone, and the web UI on a phone. The owner attaches from the desktop app.
- A worker on a second remote host; a worker surviving an upgrade.
- Delegation and the sandbox.
- The built-in assistant acting on any pane; the workspace overview.
- The nocx server in a browser.
- A pane floating over its tab (picture-in-picture), and dragging one tab onto another —
  both are 0.6.
- The 110 terminal-area bugs deferred on 2026-09-17. They come back only when the owner
  hits one again, as a child of nocx-fxzot.1.
- Commands surviving a restart (nocx-rtg0), shell integration tier A (nocx-5mn) and the
  semantic command line (nocx-w7h): deferred by this charter, each with nothing open left
  in it.

## Finding budget

**20** — `findingBudget` in `.githooks/backlog-gate/config.json`. Nine bugs were filed in
the milestone's first two days, most of them from agent messaging, so twenty is about one
more week at that pace. Past it, a new finding waits for 0.6 or displaces something only
by the owner's explicit decision. If the full working-day runs surface many small defects,
the budget runs out sooner and that decision comes sooner — which is the point of it.
