# 0.6 — The working day runs in nocx, and nocx paints its own terminal

Charter, approved by the owner on 2026-09-28 (nocx-q8yjf.17). Status is never kept here —
ask the tracker: `scripts/feature-status.sh <feature>`.

## What ships

The owner's working day runs in nocx with herdr uninstalled, the terminal is painted by
nocx from the backend's cells rather than by xterm.js.
