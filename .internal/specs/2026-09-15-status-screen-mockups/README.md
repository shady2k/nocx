# Status screen mockups (2026-09-15)

Concept images for the status screen (`nocx-ss9gh`): a screen in nocx that answers "where
are we across my projects, why is this stuck, and what are the agents doing". They were
generated by codex (gpt-6-astra) from the two briefs kept beside them, during the
brainstorm `nocx-ss9gh.1.2`. They are concepts to choose from, not a specification: the
spec that follows them is the binding document.

The model they were drawn within is recorded in the epic `nocx-ss9gh`: a project is a
repository identified by its origin, its backlog is the `br` database on its coordinator's
host, workers never write the tracker, no canvas, no central server.

## Data

Titles, stage counts and `blocks` edges for the feature "Replace herdr" are real, taken
from `real-data-feature-status.txt` (`scripts/feature-status.sh herdr`) and
`real-data-stage-graph.json`. The projects iaam and oh-my-portal, event times, "stale"
items and the whole agent wave in round 2 are illustrative. Per-epic task counts were not
supplied, so graph nodes show "— / —" rather than invented numbers.
