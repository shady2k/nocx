## Scope frozen, and 0.6 releases without the working day (owner, 2026-10-19)

2026-10-19: "рабочий день без herdr сейчас не будет выполнен. Предлагаю сейчас перестать
расширять веху и зарелизить. А herdr возьмем в следующую веху" — the working day in nocx
will not be done now, so stop widening the milestone, release it, and take herdr into the
next one.

- **Outcome 1 moves to 0.7**, whole: the herdr stage `nocx-xn63t.6` and its DONE WHEN
  "one full working day runs in nocx with herdr stopped" (`nocx-xn63t.6.2`) now belong to
  0.7, together with every open leaf under it. What is DELIVERED and on main is what those
  leaves built — orchestration, worker spawn and close, the coordinator's mailbox, the
  cell-painted terminal — and the DONE WHEN that judges it is 0.7's criterion now.
- **Outcome 2 stays in 0.6** and is delivered: the client paints cells, xterm.js is gone,
  and its stage `nocx-zg3k3.15` was accepted by the owner on 2026-10-19.
- **Outcome 3 stays in 0.6** and is delivered by the release itself: the brew cask and the
  Linux installer ship with v0.6.0.
- Every open 0.6 leaf that is not in flight was relabelled `v0-7` and deferred with a
  2026-11-15 review date, so it comes back whole in the next milestone rather than being
  dropped or silently re-scoped. The two exceptions still open and IN FLIGHT are
  `nocx-zg3k3.14.26` (the running block that breaks after its first screenful, taken into

### Native fish integration (owner request, 2026-10-21)

After exercising the installed application, the owner explicitly requested fish
support (`nocx-sd8uk`). This admits that named feature as planned work, not another
rendering finding: a person whose real login shell is fish can use the integrated
command editor, command blocks, cwd and real exit statuses in an ordinary
helper-hosted pane, without substituting Bash or changing their configuration.
Its check is the real-fish helper/backend/browser happy path and the rebuilt
native application; startup and channel-loss checks must also preserve a usable
native fish prompt. The existing conventional fallback on a host without a
helper is not claimed as enhanced remote-fish support.

The prior scope freeze otherwise stands. This request changes neither the
finding budget nor the deferred working-day criterion and authorizes no release
or merge.
